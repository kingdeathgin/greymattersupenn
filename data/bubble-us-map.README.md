The US state outlines in `bubble-us-map.json` were derived from:
https://github.com/PublicaMundi/MappingAPI/blob/master/data/geojson/us-states.json

Coordinates use a simple local projection with separate Alaska and Hawaii insets.
Markers indicate a home state, not a student's precise location. The dataset covers
all 50 states and Washington, DC. No third-party map service receives submissions.
