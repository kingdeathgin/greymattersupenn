# Cutouts-only PowerPoint

Open `grey-matters-cutouts.pptx`. Every visible item is a separate selectable picture. Copy/paste pieces into another presentation. Slide backgrounds are white; cutouts retain their transparent pixels or native shaped boundaries. No presentation text, portraits or flattened slide screenshots are included.

1. Isolated motor protein and cargo. Transparent PNG adapted from the existing watercolor illustration. Built-in imagegen extraction; original artist credited in the main deck. Select the image to copy, move or resize.

2. Nine independently selectable paper cutouts from public/images/editorial/neuroscience-margin-notes.png: neuron, brain, synapse, tape, colored marks and arrows. Original transparency preserved; cropping is native to PowerPoint.

3. Brush-edge picture cutouts: Thinking in Tongues and The Accelerating Clock. Artwork: Elgin Tawiah.

4. Three independent diagonal picture strips from Written in our genes? Artwork: Elgin Tawiah. Each strip can be selected and moved separately.

5. Wave-shaped art panels: synaptic illustration and chromosome/DNA illustration from the website archive. Native editable picture geometry.

6. Paint-shaped art cutouts: The Truth Behind Intelligence, Feeling Our Age, and the Newton illustration. Artwork from the Penn Grey Matters archive.

7. Paint-shaped art cutouts: intelligence framework, brain/tree illustration, and The Shrinking Brain. Artwork from the Penn Grey Matters archive.

8. Watercolor paint borders from the issue background archive. Each border is an independent picture with a native clipped edge.

9. Eight individually selectable paint droplets, using archive artwork and watercolor textures.
